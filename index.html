What's slowing logistics today

01

Terrain lock-out

High-altitude and monsoon-cut routes strand ground convoys for days, forcing posts to stockpile against uncertainty rather than resupply on demand.

02

Personnel exposure

Foot-carried and convoy resupply puts personnel on exposed routes for tasks that don't need a person on that route at all.

03

Import dependency

Reliance on externally sourced platforms ties spares, upgrades, and software into a supply chain the operator doesn't control.

04

Slow last-mile response

Medical, ration, and equipment resupply for isolated outposts can lag well behind the moment the need is identified.
